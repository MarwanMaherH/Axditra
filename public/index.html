How it works

Four steps. One sitting.

This is a real sequence — each step depends on the one before it, and each one is designed to shorten the distance to your first "it's fixed."

01

Paste your URL — no sign-up

We scan the live page against WCAG criteria and surface every issue, ranked by real-world impact.

02

Get a transparent score

Every score comes with the math behind it. No black box — you can see exactly why a page scored 91 and not 100.

03

Read the plain-language explanation

AI explains what's actually wrong and who it affects — the tool doubles as a teacher, not just a checker.

Builds understanding, not just a checklist
04

Copy the fix, or open a Pull Request

Take the corrected code straight into your editor, or let Axditra open the PR for you.
